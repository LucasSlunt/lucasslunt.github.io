Apple graph

Two nodes are connected if they are similar.

  • Texture: Round corners indicate soft apples, while sharp corners indicate crispness.
  • Sweetness: Indicated by the inner colour; bright pink is sweet, while lighter pinks are less so.
  • Sourness: Bright thick outlines indicate a strong sour taste, while thinner, darker outlines are for apples which are less tart.
  • Size: Larger icons represent larger apples, and smaller icons represent smaller apples.
  • Juiciness: A strong shadow indicates a juicier apple, a lack of shadow reveals a dry apple.
